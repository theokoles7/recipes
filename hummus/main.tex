            Dry Chickpeas (Garbanzo Beans)  & 1 pound       & Typically 1 bag   \\
            Tahini                          & 2 cups        & Typically 1 jar   \\
            Lemons                          & 4             & Ideally large     \\
            Garlic                          & 2 bulbs       & Ideally large     \\
            Cumin                           & 2 teaspoons   &                   \\
            Extra Virgin Olive Oil          & 1 cup         &                   \\
            Salt                            & 3 teaspoons   & Finely ground     \\
            Baking Soda                     & 1 teaspoon    &                   \\
            \hline

    \begin{multicols}{2}

    \section{Preparation}

        \subsection{Soaking the Chickpeas}

            In a large mixing bowl/pot, submerge the dried chickpeas in water with $\frac{1}{2}$ teaspoon of baking soda. Cover the bowl/pot with foil or clinging plastic wrap, and leave it to soak for approximately 12 hours.

        \subsection{Juicing the Lemons}

            Juice all four lemons completely. This should result in approximately 1 cup of fresh squeezed lemon juice.

    \section{Cooking}

        \subsection{Boiling the Chickpeas}

            Drain the chickpeas from the bowl that they are soaking in. Submerge them in a pot of water, ensuring that the water level is approximately 2 inches above the chickpeas. Add in a $\frac{1}{2}$ teaspoon of baking soda. Bring the chickpeas to a boil, then lower the heat and let them simmer for 1 hour.

        \subsection{Roasting the Garlic}

            Preheat an oven to 350$^\circ$F. For each bulb of garlic, chop approximately 1 inch off the top of the bulb such that it exposes each clove of garlic. Wrap each bulb loosely in a tin foil pocket. Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with salt and pepper. Close the wrap, set them on a tray, and roast in the oven for 1 hour.

    \section{Blending}

        It is important to note that due to the amount of chickpeas, it may be best/safer to blend the humms in two batches, each according to the following 3 stages:

        \begin{enumerate}
            \item Start with $\frac{1}{2}$ cup lemon juice, 6 cloves of garlic, and 1 teaspoon of salt
            \item Add 1 cup of Tahini
            \item Add in half of the chickpeas, 1 teaspoon of cumin, $\frac{1}{4}$ cup of olive oil, $\frac{1}{2}$ cup of water from the boiled chickpeas, and a couple of ice cubes.
        \end{enumerate}

    \section{Finishing \& Serving}

        Pour the blended hummus into a container of appropriate size. Let the container(s) rest in the freezer for approximately 1 hour, or until it settles into being more dense. When the prefered consistency is reached, pour a small amount of olive oil on top and garnish with the leftover roasted garlic with a sprinkle of smoked paprika, cumin, and parsely.

    \end{multicols}
